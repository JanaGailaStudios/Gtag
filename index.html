Gorilla Sandbox

Headset (Quest / WebXR): press Enter VR. Plant your hands on surfaces and push or pull to move. Let go to launch. Thumbstick left/right snaps your turn. A/X or B/Y respawns you.

Desktop: click to capture the mouse. Left click / Right click reach with the left / right hand. Hold a button and move the mouse to pull or push (drag down to reach the floor). Scroll to extend or retract. Mouse moves the camera while no hand is held. Shift / C crouch (needed to reach the floor). Q / E turn, R respawns, Esc releases the mouse.
